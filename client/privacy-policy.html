Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026 · Effective immediately

"Speak. Interrupt. Adapt."

1. Introduction

Welcome to VOXA ("VOXA," "we," "us," or "our"), an adaptive, voice-first AI assistant designed to streamline student daily digital workflows. VOXA enables users to interact through spoken natural language, seamlessly connect essential academic services—including Google Calendar, Google Classroom, and Gmail—and perform safe browser companion actions via the VOXA Chrome Extension.

We respect your privacy and are committed to transparency. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we process, how that data is used to provide requested features, how we safeguard your information, and how you maintain complete control over your accounts and permissions.

Core Privacy Principle: VOXA only requests the minimal read-only permissions required to answer your voice questions and coordinate your schedule. We do not sell your personal data, nor do we use your personal content to train public AI models.

2. Information We Collect

VOXA collects and processes information strictly necessary to fulfill your spoken voice requests and integration workflows:

  • Voice and Speech Input: Spoken commands captured through your browser's microphone when voice recognition is activated.
  • Connected Account Identifiers: Your Google user email address used to identify connected state and display authenticated badges.
  • Academic and Schedule Data: Calendar events, Classroom courses, coursework titles, and recent email metadata retrieved in response to your explicit voice queries.
  • Browser Companion Context: Active tab URLs and page titles reported by the VOXA Chrome Extension to support browser navigation actions.
  • Technical Session Diagnostics: Ephemeral latency timestamps, request IDs, and connection health states utilized to manage voice interruption and recovery.

3. Voice and Speech Data

VOXA uses the browser's built-in Web Speech API (speech-to-text) to capture audio locally in your browser. Audio streams are converted to text queries on the client side:

  • VOXA does not record or store raw ambient audio recordings on our servers.
  • Voice input is processed only when you explicitly start a session via "Start Talking" or the spacebar shortcut.
  • Microphone capture immediately pauses or stops when you click "Stop Voice", navigate away, or close the application.
  • Synthesized spoken responses returned to you are streamed as binary audio (audio/mpeg) generated in real time through Rime TTS.

4. Google Account Data

When you choose to link your Google account to VOXA, you authorize access via Google's official OAuth 2.0 consent screen. We only request read-only scopes necessary to summarize your academic day:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email — Used solely to verify identity and display your connected account email address in the dashboard.

We do not request write access to your Google account, nor do we request access to your Google passwords. Your login occurs directly on Google's secure authentication servers.

5. Google Calendar Data

Under scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly:

  • VOXA reads scheduled event summaries, start/end timestamps, locations, and descriptions.
  • Calendar data is fetched on-demand when you ask schedule questions (e.g., "What do I have tomorrow?" or "What is my next meeting?").
  • We do not create, modify, delete, or share your calendar entries.

6. Google Classroom Data

Under scopes https://www.googleapis.com/auth/classroom.courses.readonly and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/classroom.coursework.me.readonly:

  • VOXA queries enrolled courses, assignment names, due dates, and submission statuses.
  • This enables VOXA to answer voice inquiries like "Any assignments due this week?" or provide direct links when you say "Open my physics homework."
  • VOXA cannot submit assignments, change grades, or alter coursework.

7. Gmail Data

Under scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly:

  • VOXA reads recent email headers (sender, subject line, date) and brief snippets to summarize your inbox when you ask (e.g., "Do I have any important emails?").
  • VOXA does not read attachment files, does not download your full email archive, and cannot draft, send, archive, or delete emails.
  • Email summaries are processed ephemerally in server memory during your query and are not permanently archived in external databases.

8. Chrome Extension Data

The optional VOXA Companion Chrome Extension operates under Manifest V3:

  • It maintains a secure local connection to your VOXA instance via Server-Sent Events (SSE) and periodic heartbeats.
  • It queries the active browser tab URL and title strictly to support commands like "What tab is open?" or to bring existing Gmail/Classroom tabs into focus.
  • It executes only predefined safe navigation actions (opening validated https:// links to Google services or assignments).
  • The extension blocks unsafe protocols (such as javascript:, file:, or data:) and never transmits your browsing history, keystrokes, or page contents.

9. How We Use Data

We use the processed information strictly to:

  1. Generate relevant, natural spoken voice answers through our LLM and Rime TTS pipeline.
  2. Synthesize cross-service student agendas combining Calendar events and Classroom deadlines.
  3. Execute voice-prompted browser navigation (e.g., opening Gmail or assignment URLs in Chrome).
  4. Power VOXA's interruption and recovery engine, discarding stale responses when you interrupt.
  5. Display accurate service connection statuses in the VOXA dashboard.

We never use or sell personal data for advertising, marketing, profiling, or data brokerage.

10. How We Store Data

OAuth tokens received from Google are stored in a restricted local data file on the hosting server (data/google_tokens.json) with restrictive filesystem permissions. Tokens are used strictly to sign authorized API requests to Google.

VOXA does not operate external third-party tracking databases or cross-site analytical trackers. Session states and conversation history are maintained in ephemeral server and browser memory for the duration of your active voice session.

11. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security safeguards to protect your personal data:

  • HTTPS / TLS Encryption: All communications between your browser, our servers, Google APIs, and Rime TTS are strictly encrypted in transit using TLS/HTTPS.
  • Server-Side Secret Isolation: Google client secrets, Rime API keys, and LLM credentials are strictly confined to backend environment variables and are never transmitted to client JavaScript.
  • CORS Whitelisting: Cross-Origin Resource Sharing is enforced, permitting access only from authorized VOXA origins.
  • Version Control Protection: All token files, credentials, and environment configurations are excluded from version control systems.

12. Third-Party Services

To deliver our voice assistant features, VOXA interacts with the following trusted third-party providers:

  • Google APIs (Google LLC): For Calendar, Classroom, Gmail, and OAuth 2.0 authentication.
  • Rime TTS (Rime Labs Inc.): For ultra-low-latency neural speech synthesis. Text snippets to be spoken are sent to Rime's synthesis API; Rime returns binary audio (audio/mpeg).
  • Google Gemini / OpenAI: For conversational natural language understanding and tool orchestration. Spoken queries and relevant retrieved academic snippets are provided to generate friendly spoken answers.
  • Render (Render Services, Inc.): Cloud hosting platform that provides the secure container infrastructure and HTTPS termination for the VOXA web service.

13. Google API Data Usage (Limited Use Policy)

Google API Services User Data Policy:
VOXA's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • Google user data is used solely to provide or improve user-facing features prominent in the VOXA interface.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties, unless necessary to provide or improve user-facing features, comply with applicable laws, or as part of a merger/acquisition with explicit consent.
  • We do not use Google user data for serving advertisements, including personalized, re-targeted, or interest-based advertising.
  • We do not permit humans to read Google user data unless we have obtained your affirmative consent for specific messages, it is necessary for security purposes (such as investigating a bug or abuse), or to comply with applicable law.

14. Data Retention

We adhere to minimal data retention practices:

  • OAuth Refresh Tokens: Retained on the secure host only as long as your Google account remains connected.
  • Calendar, Classroom & Gmail Content: Fetched on-demand and retained in temporary volatile memory only for the duration of query execution and conversational turn response. Not permanently archived.
  • Conversation History: Maintained in client/browser session state and can be immediately wiped at any time using the "Clear" button.

15. User Control and Data Deletion

You maintain complete authority over your information at all times:

  • Clear Conversation: Click the "Clear" button in the conversation panel to erase active transcript records.
  • Revoke Permissions: You can disconnect your Google account directly within VOXA at any time by clicking "Disconnect" in the dashboard.
  • Google Account Permissions Manager: You can also review and revoke VOXA's access at any time through your Google Account Security Permissions page.
  • Data Deletion Requests: To request complete deletion of any stored tokens or configuration records, contact us using the information below.

16. Disconnecting Google Account

When you click "Disconnect" in the VOXA dashboard:

  1. VOXA sends an immediate revocation request to Google's token revocation endpoint.
  2. All stored access and refresh tokens are permanently unlinked and deleted from local storage (data/google_tokens.json).
  3. In-memory authentication credentials are wiped, and integration statuses reset immediately to "Not Connected".

17. Children's Privacy

VOXA is designed as a productivity tool for students and adult learners. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information directly from children under the age of 13 without verified parental or institutional consent. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data without required consent, please contact us immediately so we can promptly delete the information.

18. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements to our features, service integrations, or legal obligations. When updates occur, the "Last updated" date at the top of this document will be revised. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
